# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

REAL contributors, maintainers, and users commit to a respectful, harassment-free community regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, constructive, and precise.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against people reporting a concern.

## Enforcement

Report community concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participation when conduct violates these expectations. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as possible.
